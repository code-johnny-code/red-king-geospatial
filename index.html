How projects run

Practical first. Maintainable always.

The goal is not to sell a complicated GIS stack. It is to leave you with cleaner data, a clearer process, and a solution your organization can keep using after the engagement ends.

  1. 01
    Define the real problem.

    Start with the decision, workflow, or failure point—not a predetermined technology.

  2. 02
    Inspect the data.

    Identify quality issues, assumptions, gaps, and the systems that depend on them.

  3. 03
    Build the smallest durable solution.

    Automate what matters, document the workflow, and avoid unnecessary dependencies.

  4. 04
    Deliver something usable.

    Clear outputs, reproducible work, and an understandable handoff.

About

Work is led by a GISP-certified geospatial professional with experience building and maintaining enterprise spatial workflows, web mapping applications, PostGIS-backed systems, address-data validation, QGIS workflows, and automation.

The practice is designed for organizations that need senior GIS capability for a defined problem, a difficult dataset, or a project that needs to move forward without adding a permanent position.
